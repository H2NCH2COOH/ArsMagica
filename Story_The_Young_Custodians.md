# The Young Custodians, a Ars Magica Introductory Story

## Synopsis

A short story that focuses on the long term plays of Ars Magica. Players start
with creating a very simple child characters whom joins a covenant as
apprentices, and are guided through years of apprenticeship by completing tasks
in traditional role-playing way, and advancing the character in the
season-by-season way.

## Major Facts

### Covenant Location

The covenant is built on the island Pianosa, the smallest and farthest one
amongst the Tremiti islands off the east coast of Italy. Pianosa island is very
small, and mostly made of rock. It is only inhabited by the covenant personnel.

Some small structures are built upon the island, but they are not in heavy use.

The covenant can be reached by small boats, and through a small and hard to spot
cave, the boat can be docked, and people can access the underground artificial
cave systems that houses the covenant proper.

### Covenant Occupants

- Lentitus: The old magus that is the only working magus in the covenant. He is
a very powerful Magus Bonisagi with blatant Gift. He is a Creo master, but his
magical capabilities are greatly hindered by the lack of Vis after the accident.
He cannot solve the problems the covenant is facing by himself. He can teach any
Art with a source quality of 15.
- Sonia: The old lady that has been teaching in the covenant for many years.
She's very good at teaching, and teaches Latin, Artes Liberales, Philosophiae,
and even Magic Theory. She has Second Sight but not the Gift, and is given a
very powerful Longevity Ritual. She has a base teaching source quality of 17.
- About 20 more covenfolks, half of which are fishermen.

## Play

The play process mainly composes of rules explanations out-of-game,
conversations in-game, seasonal activities and advancements, plus some
traditional "real-time-role-playing".

The whole game takes a very simulationist approach, and focuses on displaying
the long-term play of Ars Magica. This asks for a more proactive sort of
players, which might not be available.

The game should be mostly made with players coming up solutions, and a short
"scenes" of their solutions working. If possible, checks should be avoided, and
as long as the players plans are sound, they should succeed.

### Act 2 - The Accident

The player characters are each taken to the covenant by a different magus. They
arrive days before the winter solstice of 1205 and are each constrained within
a small room without any contact except the magus. On the solstice, they are
dressed in plain white robe and taken into an underground chamber. A ritual
begin with their respective parens writing strange runes onto their robes, while
chanting arcane words and burning incenses. A strange ringing suddenly filled
the chamber without source and all the parens fell to the ground unconscious.
The player characters feels a strange power engulfed them. Lentitus quickly
enters the chamber, and begins examining the situation. This is the first time
the player characters meet him. To them, Lentitus is a very old man, yet without
the feebleness of the age. He satisfies almost all stereotypical ideas of evil
sorcerer. However, this feeling is caused by his blatant Gift. He guides the
player characters back to the main hall and asks them to remain there. About an
hour later, he comes back and sits down with the player characters. He then
casts a simple CrMe spell to make the player characters trust him, then touched
them in turn to extend his Parma Magica. The distrust and fear vanishes as the
Parma comes, and Lentitus explains that the situation is complicated by an
unexpected accident, but their apprenticeship will begin normally.

The player characters are then brought back to their rooms to rest. In the
following day, they are introduced to the rest of the covenant, and given daily
schedules for the coming apprenticeship.

### Act 3 - Apprenticeship Begins

The player characters spend their first year of their apprenticeship learning
Artes Liberales (17 exp) and Latin (51 exp) from Sonia.

Before the new year of 1207, Lentitus gathers the player characters and explains
the road ahead. The key points are:

- The standard process of apprenticeship.
- Lentitus will have to open the Arts of all of the player characters.
- Lentitus will not have enough time to teach all of them, especially Arts.
- The above is probably illegal in the Order.
- The player characters might need to be allowed to teach themselves from books.
- The player characters might be allowed to use their parens labs.
- The covenant is facing many problems going forward which must be resolved.

Lentitus then explains that he will begin opening Arts the next year and that
will take most of his time. The player characters will need to take up the
responsibility of saving the covenant.

Starting spring 1207, the player characters take turns having their Arts opened.
Each season, one player character will have the Arts opened, and the rest will
take 3 seasons of Magic Theory lessons from Sonia (50 exp), and at least two
seasons of self learning using *Aristotle's Physics* (Philosophiae Summa,
Level 6, Quality 10).

This stage ends when all player characters have had their Arts opened, and all
of them have finished the 5 or more seasons of studying.

During this first stage of apprenticeship, the player characters are not allowed
to meet anyone from outside the covenant. However, they do notice that:

- The covenant folks leave the island every Sunday to go to a chapel on the
mainland.
- A redcap visits the covenant for a day on early autumn each year.

### Act 4 - Take up the Burden

After all the player characters have had their Arts opened, and finished at
least 5 seasons of learning (3 Magic Theory + 2 Philosophiae), at the end of the
season, they are gathered by Lentitus to discuss the problems the covenant is
facing:

- Normal Aegis Vis cost.
    - The Aegis of the Hearth requires Vis each year and this is currently
    covered by the Vis stock.
    - If Lentitus spend a single season each year extracting Vim Vis, it can
    cover the cost.
- Normal Lab usage money cost.
    - The labs of the parens of the player characters are maintained but not
    used at the moment, and Lentitus's lab is in active use.
    - Each active lab will require buying various materials and tools.
- Normal covenant personnel money cost.
    - About 20 covenfolks, 1 mage, and the player characters are supported by
    the covenant, including food, drink, cloth, and various other costs.
    - These used to be covered selling magic services by magi.
- The covenant coffer will be emptied within several years if left at current
state.

Also, based on actual play, the following problems can happen at any appropriate
time:

- Magical protection against storms.
    - The entrance in the cave is protected from sea water by magic.
    - The small structures above ground are not protected by magic.
    - If the players decided to utilize the island ground, storm might become
    problematic.
- Trouble from Abbey of Santa Maria a Mare.
    - The abbey is on San Nicola, the largest one amongst the Tremiti Islands.
    - The abbey owns the islands and lands on the mainland.
    - The abbey is effectively independent at the time of the story, but in
    decline.
    - The abbey's independence was gained with the help of the covenant more
    then a century ago, and as a result, the covenant is left alone. But this
    agreement is not known by the current monks.
    - The abbey might decided to do something about the "heretics".
- Magical services/items requirements.
    - The magi might have ongoing contracts/favors that entails performing
    magical services or creating new magic items.
    - The player characters might be able to fulfill them.
- Investigation pressure from Quaesitor.
    - The Quaesitor might notice the absence of the magi, especially after
    missing both Tribunals at 1207 and 1214.
    - A Quaesitor might come to visit.
    - The Quaesitor knows about the player characters being taken in as
    apprentices, and might become suspicious.
- Magical creatures.
    - A sea serpent with Might 10 might have taken the Vis sources under sea for
    itself, eating the Vis.

Lentitus will suggest the following possible solutions:

- If he spend all of his time extracting Vim Vis, it should cover the cost of
Aegis and selling the rest should cover the rest.
- If Creo or Form Vis can be sourced each year, he can invent new ritual to
create valuables to satisfy the money requirements.
    - Once the required Vis is ready, a season should be enough to invent a new
    ritual.
    - The covenants Vis sources are all undersea, and can't be harvested by
    mundane means.
- If the player characters extract Vis, Lentitus can be freed.
    - Lentitus can extract 5-6 pawns of Vis each season.

Here's some other possible solutions that Lentitus might suggest:

- Mundane activities.
    - The player characters might provide income through mundane works, but this
    will impact their advancement as magi, and their labor are not particularly
    valuable.
    - All of them working a full year can offset one season of Lentitus's work.
- Magic enhanced mundane activities.
    - Fishing, farming, etc.
    - He is of no direct help, and must be done by the player characters.

Overall, Lentitus suggests that the player characters to focus on improving
their capabilities first before trying to solve problems.

#### Books

These books that are available:

- Creo summa, level 6, quality 21
- Intellego summa, level 6, quality 21
- Muto summa, level 6, quality 21
- Perdo summa, level 6, quality 21
- Rego summa, level 6, quality 21

Other books might also be available if the player characters are given the
access of the covenant library.

#### Lab Texts

All standard spells of level 15 or lower are available. If the players come up
with good idea of a spell which would be impractical for them to invent, similar
spell might already exist as lab text.

#### Lab Assistance

One player character can act as an assistant to another. This would provide a
large bonus to the lab total, but the assistant gains only exposure and nothing
else. One redeeming thing is that the assistant can read the resulting lab text
thus being able to quickly reproduce the result.
