Description:
    As the first child of a ship builder family in Sicily, John was born with nothing beneath his knees. Unable to walk, he was kept at the workshop for most of his childhood. Fortunately, John grew up without other problems, and with nothing else to do, began helping in the workshop. Strapped onto a chair and moving around using two sticks, John shown much talent for woodwork, but considering his disability, he only worked inside on small items. At the age of 15, John was discovered by a Verditius maga named Spesia, and took as her apprentice. Then at the age of 30, John passed his gauntlet by enchanting the Walking Chair and earned his Hermetic name: Baculus.
