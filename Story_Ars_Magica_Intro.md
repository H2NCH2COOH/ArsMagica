# Intro to Ars Magica

## Overview

A short story aimed to introduce Ars Magica to new players.

The whole experience shall be divided into 4 acts.
Each act shall introduce the players with elements of Ars Magica, including settings and rules.

The players will be given preset characters and follow a somewhat predetermined story.

## Story Outline

- A group of mundane folks are attacked by goblins and saved by a group of magi and companions.
- Magi and companions investigate the goblins and are foiled by a faerie champion.
- They return to the covenant to discus what to do next, and take a season to prepare.
- They enter the court of the faerie queen and solve the problem.

## Act I

This act will introduce the following:

- Mythic Europe in the eyes of normal mundane people.
- Simple and stress dice.
- Attributes and abilities.
- Basic combat rules.
- Existence of Hermetic Magi and their covenants.
- The time required to heal wounds.

### The Start

Give the players each a common folk character sheet.

Introduce the year of 1220 and the location of southern Germany.

The PCs are returning from a near by town to their own small village at the other end of the forest.

Encourage the players to ask about the situation and engage rolls with abilities.

### The Attack

6-8 goblins suddenly emerges from the forest and attacks.

Engage combat and explain rules, especially wound and fatigue.

The goblins avoid killing and try to capture.

The goblins can be conversed, but they are not talkative.

### The Rescue

The PCs are captured, with wounds.

The goblins lead them into the forest.

After about an hour, they hear strange chanting from the left and a lightning bolt strikes the leading goblin.
Then as the goblins turned to the left, a large man with a full set of armour and a very large sword suddenly appears in the right and strikes down a goblin.
The rest of the goblins flee into the forest.

A magus appears from the trees and the warrior checks upon the PCs.
If any PC is wounded, the magus casts a spell that binds the wounds.

After everyone is treated, the magus and the warrior decide to take the PCs back to the covenant to properly treat the wounds.

On the way, the warrior tries to comfort the PCs and would be happy to answer any questions.
The magus on the other hand, tries to keep to him self.

The group goes deep into the forest following a secret path, until they reach a clearing with fields, huts, houses, and high towers.
The PCs are led into a house and told to rest until their wounds heal.
Later, a old maga entered and cast a spell on each one of them to for Recovery bonus.
The warrior then escorts anyone that is only lightly wounded back to the village.
If none of them are only lightly wounded, the warrior asks for some tokens that he can bring back to the village to deliver the message of the situation.

### Facts

- The village is relatively new, only two generations.
- The village is built upon a very small hunter gather settlement, whose residents have either been converted or fled.
- The village's founding is led by the local nobility and the Church.
- Trees were cut down, earth was worked, buildings of stone were erected, including a chapel.
- The road has been safe from faeries for years.
- The forest has been dangerous, but is becoming safer each year.
- Stories about the danger of the forest is well known in the village.
- Goblins:
  - Live in caves under ground.
  - Less and less chance to harass human as the village became more and more secure, and people avoiding forests.
  - The reduction of vitality source forced them to attack the PCs on the road.
  - They plan to eat the PCs.
- The Magus and the Warrior:
  - The magus was christened as Timothy.
  - The warrior is called Hans.
  - They're on their way back from collecting Vis.
  - They're not aware of any previous faerie attack on the road against multiple people.
  - They're relatively new members of the covenant.
  - The magus has just finished his gauntlet last year.
  - The warrior is a mercenary with supernatural powers.

## Act II

This act will introduce the following:

- Supernatural powers.
- Formulaic spells.
- Group combat.
- Magic Resistance and Penetration.

### The Start

Give the players the following character sheets:

- Timothy the Magus: A magus Bonisagi who's good with Creo and Auram.
- Hans the Sword: A master swordsman with full armour, two handed sword, and the power of teleportation.
- Karl the Shield: A captain leading a group of 3 grogs, all armoured and wielding sword and shield.
- Anna the Hunter: A hunter who can navigate the forest with supernatural capabilities.

## Act III

This act will introduce the following:

- Covenant structure.
- Hermetic Order codes and politics.
- Wound healing.
- Long term activities and lab activities.
